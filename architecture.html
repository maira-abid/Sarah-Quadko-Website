    Home /Architecture

    Buildings shaped by site, light and material.

    Quadko's architectural practice treats every commission as a spatial argument for how a family lives, how a business receives its clients, and how a building meets its climate. The work is quiet, material-led, and built to last past trend cycles.

    Architecture placeholder

    What we deliver

    Concept & Feasibilitytoggle
    Site analysis, massing studies and early design concepts that test an idea against budget and buildable reality before full commitment.
    Design Development
    Plans, sections and elevations resolved in detail, including material palettes, structural coordination, and services integration.
    Construction Documentation
    Drawing sets and specifications prepared for permitting and contractor pricing, coordinated with structural and MEP consultants.
    Site Supervision
    Regular site visits through construction to protect design intent, review quality of work, and resolve issues as they arise.
    Site First

    Orientation, climate and context set the brief before a single wall is drawn.

    Material Honesty

    Materials are chosen to age well and to be seen for what they are: brick, wood, stone, plaster.

    Built to Last

    Details are resolved for the contractors and craftspeople who will actually build them.
