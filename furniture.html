    Furniture made to be lived with, not around.

    Every piece in the Quadko collection starts as an original design, then gets built by hand in small runs: solid wood, natural upholstery and metal, sized and finished to order rather than pulled off a factory line.

    Furniture placeholder

    How we make it

    Design & Prototypingtoggle
    Every piece starts as a sketch and a scale prototype, tested for proportion and comfort before a single board is cut.
    Material Selection
    Solid timber, natural fabrics and metal are chosen by hand for grain, weight and how they'll wear in over years of use.
    Joinery & Construction
    Frames are built by local craftspeople using traditional joinery, reinforced where a piece will see daily use.
    Finishing & Quality Check
    Hand-applied finishes, upholstery and hardware are fitted and inspected before a piece ever leaves the workshop.
    Solid Materials

    No veneer over particleboard: solid wood, real leather and natural fill throughout.

    Made to Order

    Every piece is built after you order, in the size and finish your space needs.

    Built to Last

    Constructed to be repaired and reupholstered, not replaced in five years.

    Common Questions

    How long does an order take?
    Most furniture ships 8–12 weeks after order confirmation, since every piece is made to order and finished by hand.
    Can a piece be customized?
    Yes. Dimensions, wood tone and upholstery fabric can all be adjusted to fit your space. Send us a photo or floor plan and we'll advise on sizing.
    Can I see material samples first?
    Yes. Wood finish and fabric swatches can be sent or viewed at the studio before you confirm an order.
    Do you ship outside Pakistan?
    Most orders are delivered within Pakistan. For international shipping, get in touch and we'll quote freight for your location.

    Have a piece in mind?

    Tell us the room, the size and the mood you're after, and we'll come back with options.
