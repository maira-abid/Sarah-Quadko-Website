    Objects designed and made by hand, in small runs.

    The same material language that shapes our furniture, scaled down to everyday objects: cheeseboards, trays, coasters and games, designed in-house and finished by the same craftspeople who build our furniture.

    Products placeholder

    How we make it

    Concept & Material Selection
    Each object starts from a material (a wood offcut, a stone, a metal) and is designed around what that material does well.
    Prototyping
    Working samples are cut and finished in-house to test proportion, weight and how a piece feels in the hand before production.
    Small-Batch Production
    Objects are produced in limited runs rather than mass manufactured, so quality doesn't slip as volume goes up.
    Finishing & Quality Check
    Every piece is sanded, oiled or sealed and inspected by hand before it's packed for delivery.
    Everyday Materials

    Solid wood, stone and metal, the same materials as our furniture, scaled down.

    Small Runs

    Produced in limited batches in-house, not ordered by the container from a factory.

    Made to Keep

    Designed as pieces you reach for daily, not ornaments that sit in a box.

    Common Questions

    Can objects be customized?
    Many pieces can be engraved, resized or made in a different wood or stone. Ask when you enquire and we'll confirm what's possible.
    Do you take bulk or corporate orders?
    Yes. Cheeseboards, coasters and similar pieces are popular as corporate gifts. Get in touch with a quantity and timeline for a quote.
    Are these sold in stores?
    Currently our objects are sold directly through the studio (by phone, WhatsApp or email) rather than through retail partners.
    Do you ship outside Pakistan?
    Most orders are delivered within Pakistan. For international shipping, get in touch and we'll quote freight for your location.
